Methodology Summary
The SHPIS framework employs Purged Walk-Forward Cross-Validation with a 5-day embargo gap to mitigate serial correlation leakage. Selective inference is restricted to high-confidence probability regimes (P > 0.85 or P < 0.15) with structural trend confirmation (ADX > 25). All models undergo a Permutation Validity Audit against shuffled labels to ensure predictive integrity and prevent algorithmic overfitting.
